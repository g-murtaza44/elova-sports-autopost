Isak twice, Wirtz once. Liverpool cruise in Bruges.

Liverpool beat Club Brugge 3-0 in the Champions League on 25 November at the Jan Breydel Stadium. Alexander Isak scored in the 13th and 82nd minutes, with Florian Wirtz scoring in between, and Liverpool are now fifth in the league phase.

Can they finish in the top eight?

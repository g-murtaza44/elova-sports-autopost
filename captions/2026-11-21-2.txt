First time in the last four. Ben Shelton is a Turin semi-finalist.

Ben Shelton beat Rafael Jodar 7-6(8), 7-5 at the ATP Finals in Turin on 19 November to reach the semi-finals of the season finale for the first time. Alexander Zverev also went through from the group after beating Arthur Fils 6-4, 7-6(6).

India piled up 720. Henry Nicholls still walked off as Player of the Match.
The first Test between New Zealand and India at the Basin Reserve, Wellington ended in a draw on 23 November. India declared on 720 for 9, their highest Test total overseas and the biggest Test total ever made in New Zealand. Nicholls made 175 not out in New Zealand's first innings of 368, and New Zealand batted out the final day on 239 for 5. The second Test starts at Hagley Oval, Christchurch on 27 November.

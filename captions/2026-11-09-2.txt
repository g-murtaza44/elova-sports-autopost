Three defeats in a row. Manchester City are in trouble.

Liam Delap scored twice, in the 18th and 73rd minutes, as Nottingham Forest beat Manchester City 3-1 at the City Ground on 7 November. Jair Cunha opened the scoring after six minutes and Rayan Cherki pulled one back for City, who are now six points behind leaders Arsenal.

Duckett 133. England take the series in Australia.

Ben Duckett hit 133 off 115 balls as England made 328 and bowled Australia out for 231 in the third ODI at Bellerive Oval, Hobart, on 18 November. The 97-run win seals the series 2-1 after England lost the opener in Perth.

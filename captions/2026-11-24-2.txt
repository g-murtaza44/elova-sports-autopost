Three set points saved. Carlos Alcaraz is the ATP Finals champion.
Alcaraz beat world No. 1 Alexander Zverev 6-2, 7-6(7) in the final at the Inalpi Arena in Turin on 22 November to win the season-ending title for the first time. He saved three set points from 6-3 down in the tie-break, one year after losing the final to Jannik Sinner.

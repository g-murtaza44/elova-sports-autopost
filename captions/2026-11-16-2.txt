Portugal crushed at home.

Rasmus Hojlund scored twice as Denmark beat Portugal 4-1 in the UEFA Nations League at the Estadio Municipal de Braga on 14 November. Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg and Joachim Andersen also scored, with Bernardo Silva getting a late goal for Portugal.

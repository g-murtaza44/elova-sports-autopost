7,000 Test runs. No Bangladesh batter had ever got there.

Mushfiqur Rahim became the first Bangladesh batter to reach 7,000 Test runs on day five of the second Test against West Indies at Sylhet International Cricket Stadium on 9 November. He made 49 before bad light ended the match in a draw with Bangladesh on 184 for 4 chasing 369, and West Indies won the series 1-0.

74 off 51 balls. A new chase king.

Virat Kohli made 74 off 51 balls as India beat New Zealand by 5 wickets (DLS) in the first ODI at Eden Park, Auckland on 4 November. Arshdeep Singh took 4 for 70 as New Zealand were bowled out for 185, and Kohli passed Sachin Tendulkar for the most runs in ODI chases with 8,741.

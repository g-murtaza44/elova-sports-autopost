First Test. Seven wickets.

Debutant fast bowler Saqib Khan took 7 for 130 as Sri Lanka were bowled out for 446 on day four of the first Test at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on 12 November. He removed both centurions, Dhananjaya de Silva (167) and Kusal Mendis (119). Pakistan closed on 57 for 1, a lead of 45.

What a start to a Test career.

242 not out. No Bangladesh batter has ever gone higher in Tests.

Towhid Hridoy finished unbeaten on 242 off 359 balls on day three of the second Test against West Indies at Sylhet International Cricket Stadium on 7 November. It is the highest Test score by a Bangladesh batter, passing Mushfiqur Rahim's 219 not out, and gave Bangladesh a 30-run first-innings lead.

Four goals, a red card and a statement win for Brentford.
Brentford beat Everton 4-0 at the Gtech Community Stadium on 23 November in the Premier League. Keane Lewis-Potter scored twice in three minutes, Everton lost James Tarkowski to a straight red card before half-time, then Vitaly Janelt and Fabio Carvalho added late goals. Brentford move up to sixth.

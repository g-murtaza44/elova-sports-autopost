A League Two side just knocked out the Premier League champions.

Fleetwood Town drew 1-1 with Arsenal at Highbury Stadium on 27 October and won 5-3 on penalties in the Carabao Cup fourth round. Aaron Loupalo-Bi scored first, Viktor Gyokeres equalised, and Jay Lynch saved from Bruno Guimaraes in the shootout.

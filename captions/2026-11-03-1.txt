Kimi Antonelli is the youngest Formula 1 world champion ever.

The 20-year-old Mercedes driver finished third at the Mexico City Grand Prix at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez on 1 November to seal the 2026 title. Max Verstappen won the race for Red Bull, 0.7 seconds ahead of Lewis Hamilton.

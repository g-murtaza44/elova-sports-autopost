Madrid belonged to the Bengals.

Joe Burrow completed 24 of 29 passes for 271 yards and 3 touchdowns, and ran in another, as the Cincinnati Bengals beat the Atlanta Falcons 34-17 at the Santiago Bernabeu in Madrid on 8 November. Ja'Marr Chase caught 10 passes for 155 yards and a touchdown as the Bengals moved to 6-2 with their first ever international win.

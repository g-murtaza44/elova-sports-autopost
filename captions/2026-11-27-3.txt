Two match points down. Italy still found a way.

Italy beat South Korea 2-1 in the Davis Cup Final 8 quarter-final in Bologna on 25 November. Andrea Vavassori and Matteo Berrettini won the deciding doubles 7-6, 7-6, taking the second tiebreak 13-11 after Korea held two match points. Italy play the Czech Republic in the semi-final today, 27 November.

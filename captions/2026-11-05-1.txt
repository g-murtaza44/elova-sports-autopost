73 off 16 balls. 11 sixes. Abbas Afridi went big in Hong Kong.

Captain Abbas Afridi smashed 73 off 16 balls as Pakistan made 154 in six overs with 22 sixes and beat Oman by 18 runs in the Plate final of the Hong Kong Sixes on 1 November. Afghanistan won the Cup final, chasing 116 against Australia in 5.1 overs.

Germany win in Belgrade.

Florian Wirtz scored in the 14th minute and Brajan Gruda added a third in the 50th as Germany beat Serbia 3-1 in the UEFA Nations League at the Rajko Mitic Stadium on 13 November. Serbia finished with nine men and are relegated from League A.

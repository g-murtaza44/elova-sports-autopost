Serbia host Germany tonight. Mitrovic is back.

Serbia face Germany in UEFA Nations League Group A2 on 13 November, kick-off 20:45 CET (00:45 Pakistan time on 14 November). Serbia are still chasing their first points of the group after Germany beat them 2-0 in October, and Aleksandar Mitrovic returns from injury. In the same group, Netherlands host unbeaten Greece.

57 seasons of waiting are over in Milwaukee.

The Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 at Tropicana Field on 28 October to win the World Series 4-1, the first title in franchise history. Catcher William Contreras was named World Series MVP after batting .381 and throwing out four baserunners.

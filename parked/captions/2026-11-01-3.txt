Love, 15, 30, 40. Why does tennis count like that?

Points go 0 (called love), 15, 30, 40, then game. At 40-40 it's deuce, and a player must win two points in a row. The first to 6 games wins the set, by two clear. At 6-6, most sets go to a tiebreak.

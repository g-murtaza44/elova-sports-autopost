China's Pei Xinyi won the women's 61 kg title at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo on 1 November 2026 with a 142 kg clean and jerk and a 254 kg total, both world records. The old marks were 139 kg by Pak Jin-hae and 251 kg by Yang Liuyue, both set at the Asian Games.

The first ever men's marathon race walk world record. 🇯🇵

Japan's Masatora Kawano walked 2:55:14 at the All Japan Race Walking meeting in Takahata on 25 October 2026. It is the first official world record in the event, which World Athletics began ratifying in 2026. The previous best was 2:55:28 by Hayato Katsuki in 2025.

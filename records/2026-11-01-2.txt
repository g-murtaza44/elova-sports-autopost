Japan rules the 200 breaststroke again. 🏊

Shin Ohashi won the men's 200m breaststroke at the Asian Games on 24 September 2026 in 2:04.83, a new world record. The old mark was 2:05.48 by China's Qin Haiyang from 2023. (Photo is representative, not the athlete.)

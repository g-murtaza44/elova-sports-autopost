390 kg in total. Nobody has lifted more at 85 kg. 🇪🇬

Egypt's Abdelrahman Younes lifted a 390 kg total in the men's 85 kg class at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo on 2 November 2026. The old world record was 379 kg by North Korea's Ro Kwang-ryol at the Asian Games in September 2026.

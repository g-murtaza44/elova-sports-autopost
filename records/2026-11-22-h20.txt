355 kg. A new best ever at 70 kg. 🇰🇵

North Korea's Pak Pyol won the men's 70 kg title at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo, China, on 30 October 2026. He set world records with 157 kg in the snatch and 355 kg in total, beating Masanori Miyamoto's 154 kg snatch and Ri Won Ju's 349 kg total.

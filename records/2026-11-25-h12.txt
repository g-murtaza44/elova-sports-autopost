126 kg in the snatch. A new best ever at 77 kg. 🇨🇳

China's Peng Cuiting snatched 126 kg in the women's 77 kg class at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo on 5 November 2026. The old world record was 123 kg by Olivia Reeves of the USA from the 2025 Worlds.

415 kg. Three world records in one night. 🇧🇬

Bulgaria's Karlos Nasar lifted 185 kg in the snatch, 230 kg in the clean and jerk and 415 kg in total in the men's 95 kg class at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo, China, on 4 November 2026. The old total record was 398 kg, set by Ali Alipour in September.

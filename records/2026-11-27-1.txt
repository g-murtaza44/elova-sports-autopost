178 kg in the snatch. A new world record at 85 kg. 🇨🇴

Colombia's Yeison Lopez snatched 178 kg in the men's 85 kg class at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo on 2 November 2026. He beat the 173 kg set by Kazakhstan's Alexey Churkin at the Asian Games in September 2026.

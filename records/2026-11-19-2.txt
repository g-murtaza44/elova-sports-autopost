Two world records in one day. 🇬🇧

Great Britain's Katie Archibald, Millie Couzens, Josie Knight and Anna Morris rode 4:03.634 in the first round of the women's team pursuit at the European Track Championships in Konya on 2 February 2026, then went faster again with 4:02.808 to beat Germany in the final. Germany had held the record since 2021.

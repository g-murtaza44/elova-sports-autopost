118 runs in six overs, without losing a wicket. 🇮🇳

India smashed 118/0 in the powerplay against New Zealand in the 3rd T20I in Wellington on 27 October 2026, with Abhishek Sharma and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi at the crease. It beat Romania's 116/0 against Serbia in 2021 as the highest powerplay score in men's T20Is.

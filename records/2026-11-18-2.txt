The first ever world record in the half marathon race walk. 🇯🇵

Japan's Toshikazu Yamanishi walked 1:20:34 at the Japanese Half Marathon Race Walking Championships in Kobe on 15 February 2026. World Athletics ratified it as the inaugural world record, beating the 1:21:30 standard.

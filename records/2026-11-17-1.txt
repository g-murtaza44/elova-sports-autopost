Four swimmers, one new world record. 🇺🇸

USA's Will Modglin, Van Mathias, Gretchen Walsh and Kate Douglass won the mixed 4x100m medley relay in 3:36.70 at the Pan Pacific Championships on 12 August 2026. They beat the 3:37.43 the USA set at the Paris 2024 Olympics.

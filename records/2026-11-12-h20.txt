Sjostrom's record lasted 9 years. Not anymore. 🇳🇱

The Netherlands' Marrit Steenbergen swam 51.68 in the women's 100m freestyle at the Sette Colli Trophy in Rome on 27 June 2026. She broke the 51.71 set by Sweden's Sarah Sjostrom at the 2017 World Championships in Budapest.

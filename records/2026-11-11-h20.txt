Three lifts, three world records. 🏋️‍♀️

North Korea's Song Kuk-hyang lifted 122 kg in the snatch, 153 kg in the clean and jerk and 275 kg in total in the women's 69 kg class at the IWF World Weightlifting Championships in Ningbo, China, on 3 November 2026. She beat her own records of 121 kg, 152 kg and 273 kg from the Asian Games.

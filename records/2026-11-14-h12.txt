A record older than she was. 🇬🇧

Great Britain's Keely Hodgkinson ran 1:54.87 in the women's 800m at the World Indoor Tour meeting in Lievin, France, on 19 February 2026. She broke the indoor world record of 1:55.82 set by Slovenia's Jolanda Ceplak in 2002, on the day Hodgkinson was born.
